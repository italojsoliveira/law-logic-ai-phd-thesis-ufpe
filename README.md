# Law, Logic and Artificial Intelligence

**Why, how, and to what extent to automate judicial dispute resolution in Brazil**

PhD thesis in Law by [Ítalo José da Silva Oliveira](https://italojsoliveira.com/), submitted to the Graduate Program in Law (PPGD) of the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE) in 2019. Concentration area: Computational Law.

This is an English version of the [original Portuguese](TESE_PPGD_UFPE.pdf) thesis.

[**Download the English translation**](EN_THESIS_PPGD_UFPE.pdf)

The thesis asks why, how, and to what extent judicial dispute resolution in Brazil should be automated. It argues that Symbolic AI can support the resolution of repetitive, simple, low-value Consumer Law cases, and it formalizes part of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code in Description Logic (SROIQ) and OWL.
